Incident Response Policy

When a production incident is detected, the on-call engineer is paged automatically via the alerting system. The on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes and begin investigation. If the on-call engineer cannot resolve the issue within 15 minutes, they must escalate to the secondary on-call and open an incident channel.

Incidents are classified into three severities. Severity 1 (SEV1) means a full outage or data loss risk affecting all customers, and requires immediate escalation to the engineering director and a public status page update within 10 minutes. Severity 2 (SEV2) means significant degradation affecting a subset of customers. Severity 3 (SEV3) means a minor issue with a workaround available.

After any SEV1 or SEV2 incident, the incident commander must schedule a blameless postmortem within 3 business days. The postmortem document must include a timeline, root cause, customer impact, and at least three concrete action items with owners and due dates.

All incidents, regardless of severity, must be logged in the incident tracker with start time, resolution time, and a short summary.
